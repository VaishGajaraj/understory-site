NISAR product opportunity map

One satellite. Different physics. Different products.

NISAR can support forests, floods, crops, moving ground, ice, and soil moisture. The opportunity is large. The disciplined move is to reuse the operating layer while giving each signal its own model, labels, uncertainty, and release gate.

GUNW + GCOVNow

Forest disturbance

Logging roads, harvest, fire, storm damage, and mining disturbance under persistent cloud—with event-level evidence and honest false-alarm accounting.

Reuse: current benchmark. Add GCOV comparison and real held-out labels.

GCOVNear

Floods and wetlands

Map open and vegetation-covered inundation, then measure duration and recurrence for response teams and wetland managers.

Reuse: AOIs, masks, provenance, GIS delivery. New water-history model and flood labels.

GCOVNear

Crop area and season

Track polarization changes through planting, growth, and harvest to support regional crop-area and food-security analysis.

Reuse: time-series plumbing. New crop-by-region model and seasonal validation.

GCOVResearch

Biomass and carbon evidence

Support forest inventory and MRV with change evidence first; biomass and carbon estimates require plots, allometry, and uncertainty calibration.

Reuse: provenance and areas. Do not convert anomaly score into carbon tonnes.

GUNW phaseSeparate

Subsidence and landslides

Measure slow line-of-sight ground motion around slopes, groundwater basins, cities, and infrastructure as expert decision support.

Reuse: inventory and delivery only. Add atmospheric correction and phase time-series inversion.

GUNW phaseSeparate

Earthquakes and volcanoes

Map deformation before and after tectonic or volcanic events. Urgent response and long-term monitoring have different latency and QA needs.

Reuse: product discovery. New event workflows, phase QA, and expert interpretation.

GOFF / ROFFSeparate

Glacier and ice motion

Use dense pixel offsets to measure large movement of glaciers, ice sheets, and sea ice where interferometric phase may decorrelate.

Reuse: operating layer only. New offset filters, geometry, and cryosphere validation.

SME2Integrate

Soil moisture and drought

Expose mission-generated field-scale soil-moisture histories for irrigation, drought, reservoir, and crop-yield workflows.

Reuse: AOIs and delivery. Consume Level 3 before inventing another retrieval algorithm.

Architecture decision

Share the operating layer. Isolate the science.

Shared core

AOIs, inventory, retrieval, caching, config versions, provenance, quality flags.

Signal adapters

GUNW coherence, GCOV backscatter, GUNW phase, GOFF offsets, SME2 values.

Application packs

Separate models, label sets, benchmarks, kill criteria, and uncertainty.

Delivery

Machine-readable reports, GIS layers, review states, and partner feedback.

The governing rule

A new market is not a new feature flag. If the physical observable, ground truth, or consequence of error changes, it is a new application pack with its own benchmark.

This keeps one failed model from contaminating unrelated claims—and lets contributors improve shared infrastructure without accepting every application’s scientific assumptions.
